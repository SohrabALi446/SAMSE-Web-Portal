NXTSM

IT Service Management & Security Workflow Automation

NXTSM bridges the gap between IT administration and security operations. It is engineered as a unified service management platform that automatically converts threat alerts, system audits, and infrastructure anomalies into actionable IT tickets, tracking them from triage to complete resolution.

Core Architecture & Service Pillars

Automated Ticketing Pipeline

Instantly transforms high-priority alerts from NXTSIEM and NXTScan into assigned tickets with zero manual intervention.

Asset Discovery & CMDB

Maintains a living inventory of software packages, network endpoints, and user sessions mapped directly to active policies.

SLA & Escalation Engine

Enforces strict Service Level Agreement timers, auto-escalating unaddressed security vulnerabilities to senior administrators.

Why NXTSM Stands Apart

Most traditional ITSM platforms (like standalone Jira or ServiceNow instances) operate as administrative silos completely detached from technical security controls. NXTSM is built natively into the SAMSE ecosystem, offering distinctive operational advantages:

01
Security-First Incident Management Instead of focusing solely on printer resets or password requests, NXTSM is purpose-built for IT administrators and security analysts, prioritizing patch deployments, firewall rule changes, and threat containment tasks.
02
Direct Integration with NXTMGR & NXTDR When an endpoint triggers an anomaly in NXTDR or a scan spots a CVE in NXTScan, NXTSM creates a ticket linked directly to the affected asset, complete with automated remediation steps that engineers can execute with a single click.
03
Change Governance & Compliance Auditing Every administrative change made to software assemblies, database records, or network routing policies is recorded and tied to an approved ticket, ensuring complete traceability for ISO and SOC 2 audits.

Value for Lean IT & Security Teams

Eliminating Manual Coordination Fatigue

In smaller teams, relying on emails or chat messages to track technical tasks leads to missed patches and overlooked alerts. NXTSM centralizes accountability so nothing falls through the cracks.

Faster Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR)

By pre-populating tickets with raw log extracts, affected IP addresses, and vulnerability diagnostic data from other SAMSE modules, technicians spend zero time investigating background context and full time fixing issues.
