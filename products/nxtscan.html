NXTSCAN

Vulnerability Management & Posture Assessment

Overview

NXTSCAN is planned as an automated vulnerability assessment and posture management engine designed to continuously scan enterprise endpoints, servers, and cloud configurations for security flaws without bulky proprietary hardware.

Planned Features

  • Automated asset discovery and scheduled credentialed scans
  • Real-time CVSS risk prioritization and reporting dashboards
