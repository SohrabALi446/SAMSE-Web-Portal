NXTSIEM

Security Information & Event Management (SIEM)

NXTSIEM is a lightweight, high-performance log collection and threat correlation engine engineered to ingest enterprise syslogs, Windows event logs, and API streams with exceptional efficiency and minimal overhead.

Basic Information & Core Specs

Log Ingestion Protocols

High-speed Syslog collection over TCP/UDP port 514, secure TLS streams, and structured API hooks.

Parsing & Normalization

Advanced custom log source parsing built on deep administrative experience with QRadar and ArcSight.

Custom Rules Engine (CRE)

Real-time behavioral anomaly detection, threshold correlation, and automated alert piping into NXTMGR.

Why NXTSIEM is Ideal for Small & Medium Enterprises (SMEs)

Zero Data-Volume Licensing Penalties

Commercial SIEM vendors charge exorbitant fees per Gigabyte of ingested logs, punishing growing businesses for collecting necessary security data. NXTSIEM features predictable, optimized pricing with zero volume tax.

Enterprise-Grade Threat Visibility

SMEs face sophisticated cyber threats just like Fortune 500 companies. NXTSIEM delivers professional rule tuning, Windows/Linux event monitoring, and rapid incident detection without requiring a 20-person SOC team.

Native Cross-Product Integration

Because NXTSIEM shares a unified core with NXTMGR and NXTDR, security alerts don't just sit in a log viewer—they actively trigger automated host isolation and firewall blocks.

Regulatory Compliance Made Easy

Easily meet compliance requirements (such as ISO 27001, SOC 2, or HIPAA) by maintaining centralized, tamper-evident audit logs and automated reporting across your entire IT environment.
