NXTGTW

Software Routing & Transit Gateway

Overview

NXTGTW is an enterprise-grade virtual routing and traffic transit gateway designed to bridge disparate cloud subnets and secure inter-network communications without physical rack routers.

Technical Highlights

  • Dynamic Layer-3 virtual routing tables
  • Instant deployment on AWS, Azure, GCP, or Proxmox VMs
  • Integrated seamlessly with NXTMGR telemetry
